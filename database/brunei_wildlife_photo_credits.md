# Starter wildlife photo credits

The catalog uses representative species photos. Only the proboscis monkey photo below is specifically identified as photographed in Brunei; other photos show the same species elsewhere. The image files are stored locally in `wildlife_api/uploads/animals/`; the Commons pages below provide the original source and license details.

Species presence and profile notes were checked against the [Brunei national biodiversity report to the Convention on Biological Diversity](https://www.cbd.int/doc/world/bn/bn-nr-05-en.pdf). The [UBD Institute for Biodiversity and Environmental Research](https://iber.ubd.edu.bn/biodiversity-information/) records hundreds of species in several animal groups and thousands of invertebrates; this starter file is therefore not a complete checklist.
